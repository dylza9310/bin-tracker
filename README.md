# 🗑️ Bin Night Schedule

A simple, live household bin collection schedule that always shows the correct week — no maintenance needed.

## What it does

- Automatically highlights **tonight’s** collection based on the current date
- Shows the **next** upcoming week and fades out past weeks
- Displays an 8-week rolling schedule
- Works on any phone browser — just open the link

## Bin rotation

|Week      |Bins                                             |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------|
|Odd weeks |🟢 Green (recycling) + 🟤 Brown (garden/food waste)|
|Even weeks|⚫ Black (general waste)                          |

Collection day: **Wednesday**. Anchor date: **20 May 2026** (green + brown week).

## How to use

Share the link in your household WhatsApp group. Anyone can open it and see what goes out this week — no app, no account, no screenshots needed.
